Evaluation lab

A toy illustration of why the attack matters as much as the defence. A linear classifier is attacked within an L∞ budget ε. The "saturated output" defence leaves every decision unchanged, but squashes the probability so a gradient-based attack sees no gradient. It looks robust until the attack is adapted.
